# Support

Maru is under active development and is not yet offered as a hosted service or
supported production package.

- Use GitHub issues for observable defects and scoped feature proposals.
- Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/martonpornoi/maru/discussions) for
  setup questions and design conversation.
- Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.
- Review [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for public-space moderation,
  GitHub's abuse-reporting route, and Maru's current lack of a private project-
  specific conduct channel.
- Consult [the development setup](docs/development/setup.md) and
  [operations documentation](docs/operations/deployment-and-service-objectives.md)
  before requesting help.

Please include the commit, operating system, Python and PostgreSQL versions,
the command you ran, and sanitized error output. Do not post secrets, access
tokens, database dumps, or personal data.

Support is best effort. The project currently promises no response or
resolution time, private product-support channel, or production-service SLA.
Issues may be redirected to Discussions, deferred when they fall outside the
maintained roadmap, or closed when the available sanitized evidence remains
insufficient to investigate safely. Intermittent and environment-dependent
defects do not require an artificial deterministic reproduction, but reports
must still exclude secrets and personal data.

# Start here

**Audience:** People evaluating Maru or entering the repository for the first
time\
**Outcome:** Understand the project, choose an exploration path, or prepare a
first contribution\
**Estimated time:** 10 minutes to understand the project; setup and the optional
tour take longer

Choose the part that matches your goal. These five pages form a guided route,
but you do not need to finish the tour before contributing a documentation
correction. The rest of the site is organized as task and reference catalogs.

## Your five-step path

1. [Understand what Maru is](what-is-maru.md). Start with furry convention
   operations and the boundaries Maru deliberately keeps.
2. [Learn what works today](current-maturity.md). Choose a current exploration
   path and understand what its evidence proves.
3. [Run Maru locally](run-locally.md). Start a disposable PostgreSQL environment
   and create a synthetic administrator.
4. [Follow a product tour](product-tour.md). Walk through fictional organizer
   setup and an event edition.
5. [Prepare a first contribution](first-contribution.md). Choose a bounded
   improvement, check it, and open a useful draft.

## Where to stop

- **Evaluating the idea?** Steps 1 and 2 are enough. Continue with the
  [product catalog](../product/index.md) if the direction fits your needs.
- **Exploring the application?** Continue through step 4, then use the
  [operations catalog](../operations/index.md) for a specialist runbook.
- **Planning to contribute?** Start at step 5; use setup and the tour when your
  change needs them. Keep the [build and contribution catalog](../development/index.md)
  nearby.
- **Looking up a symbol or historical decision?** Use
  [reference and history](../reference/index.md) or site search.

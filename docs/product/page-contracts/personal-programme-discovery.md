## Purpose and ownership

An active verified person chooses an edition from their own retained Programme
hosting or Workforce Shift purposes, without discovering or typing identifiers.
No Participation, proposal authorship, Assignment alone, suitability, general
edition directory or account existence establishes a timetable relationship.
Pending/ended retained purposes justify own history, not confirmed work or attendance.

Programme and Workforce independently supply only opaque candidate scopes and
current exact-person purpose proof. Existing Events exact-profile filters remain
closed; no current manifest is widened. The Scheduling compositor requires each
adopted personal timetable owner's independent self fields. Owner labels are read
only for an admitted actual retained purpose, using minimized Events and
Organizations references. Names and stable human codes distinguish organization,
series and edition without exposing contacts, legal records or other people.

## Complete disclosure and failure

At most 256 distinct candidate scopes per owner and 256 combined candidates are
accepted. Probe one beyond the bound and withhold the entire chooser on overflow;
never silently truncate or show hidden counts. Candidate IDs grant no authority.
Acquire all independently admitted canonical parent scopes in stable order before
the person lock. Recheck complete candidate/admission sets and owner purpose/label
facts without acquiring a new parent after the person lock. Required minimized
own-purpose audit precedes label disclosure. Compare again after final rendering.

An authorized empty chooser says only that no retained timetable purposes are
available to this person. Denied/unadopted scopes expose no label or count.
Malformed/foreign/inactive attribution is non-disclosing; incomplete, moving,
over-bound or audit-failed evidence releases no old or partial choices.
Choices do not fetch a timetable, release, invitation copy, work briefing,
availability, suitability or other-person record. The selected timetable performs
its own complete independent protected reads and audits.

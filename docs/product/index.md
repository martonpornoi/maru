# Product

**Audience:** Product evaluators, designers, domain contributors, and
implementers\
**Outcome:** Find the product intent or interface contract needed for a task\
**Reading time:** 2 minutes to choose a source

Start with the [product vision](vision.md). Then choose the narrowest source
that answers your question:

- [Personas and jobs](personas-and-jobs.md) identifies the people and outcomes
  Maru serves.
- [Key workflows](key-workflows.md) and the
  [annual lifecycle](annual-lifecycle.md) explain end-to-end work.
- [Experience and information architecture](experience-and-information-architecture.md)
  and [platform brand](platform-brand.md) define the visible experience.
- [Requirements](requirements.md) is the stable behavioral and acceptance
  contract.
- [Capability map](capability-map.md) describes the broader product horizon; it
  is not a statement that every capability exists today.
- [Page contracts](page-contracts/index.md) specify the accepted management
  surfaces and their evidence boundaries.
- [Legacy capability map](legacy-maru-capability-map.md) is behavior-level input,
  not current implementation authority.

Return to [what works today](../start-here/current-maturity.md) when a product
document does not make its implementation status clear.

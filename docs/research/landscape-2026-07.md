# Convention operations capability landscape

Status: Repository-owned synthesis
Last updated: 2026-08-22

This note records general operating problems Maru is designed to solve. It
does not profile, quote, or reproduce the structure of a named convention.
Earlier source-specific research remains available in Git history but is not
current product instruction or fixture input.

## Recurring operational patterns

Community conventions commonly need to coordinate:

- independently governed organizers and recurring event editions;
- registration, admission products, payments, badges, and check-in;
- volunteer recruitment, onboarding, qualifications, shifts, and handover;
- programme submissions, review, scheduling, rooms, technical requirements,
  and publication;
- exhibitors, charity activity, merchandise, guests, and hospitality;
- venues, accommodation, logistics, equipment custody, and live operations;
- accessible, privacy-respecting communication before, during, and after an
  event; and
- durable decisions, audit evidence, exports, recovery, and year-to-year
  continuity.

These are domain categories, not permission to copy another organization's
names, hierarchy, policies, people, or internal authority model.

## Product implications

Maru should favor explicit relationships between these workflows instead of a
collection of unrelated forms. Registration can affect entitlements and
check-in; programme decisions can affect rooms, equipment, staffing, and
publication; operational changes can affect communication and recovery.

Organizer policy remains configurable, while authority, scope, privacy,
history, and audit stay typed and reviewable. External specialist tools may be
integrated through documented adapters where replacement would add risk
without improving the coherent user journey.

## Ethical research boundary

Public material may be consulted to formulate a general requirement when the
purpose and source are reviewed. Repository-controlled examples use only
fictional Maru conventions, synthetic people, and reserved domains. Maru does
not ingest public rosters, copy people-to-role mappings, reuse convention
branding, or imply endorsement or affiliation.

Any future partner-specific research or migration requires explicit consent,
provenance, minimization, retention, access, correction, and removal rules
before data enters Maru.

# Research catalog

**Audience:** Product and architecture contributors validating assumptions\
**Outcome:** Find reviewed inputs without confusing them with requirements or
current implementation\
**Reading time:** 2 minutes plus the selected study

Research records problem framing, comparisons, and synthetic operating-model
inputs. They may inform requirements and decisions, but the current
[requirements](../product/requirements.md), accepted
[ADRs](../architecture/decisions/index.md), and
[project state](../project/CURRENT.md) remain authoritative.

Examples and source material must not copy real convention rosters, staff
handles, contact details, or branding. Maru's maintained examples use fictional
conventions such as MaruCon and MaruDance.

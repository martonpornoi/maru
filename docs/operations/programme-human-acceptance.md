## Accessibility and comprehension

On the new connected paths, include empty, populated, invalid, denied, stale,
unavailable, review, published and stopped/read-only states. Record applicable
cells; mark a genuinely inapplicable cell with a reason instead of omitting it.

- Traverse ordinary navigation, fields, errors, approvals, notice acknowledgement
  and continuity using keyboard alone: visible focus, meaningful order, no trap,
  functioning skip/jump links and predictable Enter/Space behavior.
- A representative screen-reader user follows the same tasks and encounters
  programmatic labels, error/status announcements, one main landmark and meaningful
  headings. An automated accessibility scan is supplementary, not this result.
- Check 320, 390, 768, 958, 1024, 1280 and 1920 CSS pixels, long fictional text,
  validation and themes. Distinguish a labelled timetable scrolling region from
  page-level overflow. Observe genuine browser **200% zoom**, not a resized viewport.
- Check native Cancel/Leave, actual print preview/pagination, readable private-copy
  warnings and reduced motion where affected. Preserve the maintainer's disabled
  Windows Animation effects and do not change personal settings without permission.

After the relevant cards, ask people to explain in their own words: who can see
each content layer; what incomplete readiness means; who approved versus published;
what happened to retained volunteer work; and when a printed/offline copy is unsafe
to rely on. Record misunderstandings before explaining the intended behavior.

# Retired external-roster rehearsal

Status: Retired; retained as a decision pointer
Last updated: 2026-08-22

An early local rehearsal parsed a public convention roster and could turn its
volunteer handles into local accounts. That approach is no longer compatible
with Maru's identity, privacy, or educational-data boundaries.

The parser, network URL, and compatibility management command have been
removed. No repository-controlled tutorial, fixture, screenshot, test, or
generated contract may use a real convention identity or public people
directory as example input.

## Supported replacement

Use `seed_demo_data`. It creates only repository-owned fictional conventions,
deterministic synthetic people, and reserved `.invalid` contacts. Its
organizations establish accountable representation through the real
provision, invitation, self-response, and two-controller activation services.
The platform administrator remains outside convention participation.

For an owner-led exercise, follow the
[Maru hands-on tutorial](../operations/maru-hands-on-tutorial.md). It creates a
fresh fictional `MaruCon` organization, series, edition, and independently
designed Department structure without reading a file or contacting an external
site.

## Boundary retained

Public organizational material may still inform separately reviewed product
requirements, but it must not become fixture data, copied taxonomy, account
identity, role assignment, screenshot content, or an implied affiliation.
Future organizer migrations require their own purpose, provenance, preview,
validation, access, retention, and approval contract.

Historical Git commits may retain the retired implementation and terminology.
This current-tree correction does not rewrite public Git history or alter the
facts recorded by earlier accepted decisions.

## Safety and recovery

Raw-PDF PUT retains ordinary Django CSRF. Purpose-signed bounded original actor,
organization, edition, proposal, question, versions and retry identity are integrity
evidence, not permission. Content type/encoding/declared bounds are checked before
the owning command's preflight invokes a max-plus-one reader. Scanning remains
outside database transactions; no caller-provided scanner receipt is trusted.
The PUT guarantee does not prevent proxy/ASGI buffering or ordinary framework
parsing of unsupported POST. Deployment resource limits remain a separate gate.

Only the upload/recovery page adds same-origin connections to its default-deny CSP.
After an attempted upload, selection and resend remain disabled, the input and proof
remain available, and only a deliberate body-free check can confirm the original
result. A missing result can still be in flight. History contains the signed proof,
not bytes or filenames; reload is recovery-only. Keyboard focus moves to status
when the invoking control becomes disabled, without stealing focus on completion.

Clear uses the canonical answer command and fixed routine rationale with original
versions/key and explicit confirmation. It does not delete custody/history or
override holds/backups. Errors retain the original clear proof and confirmation.
Independent current/sealed/reviewer/moderator/decider readers precede metadata and
bytes; anonymous review omits links and denies direct lookup. After preparing an
attachment with fixed filename, no-store and nosniff, the adapter repeats owner
metadata/source/authority admission and withholds the entire response on change.

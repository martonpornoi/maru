## Outcome and boundaries

Applications exact-recipient history/detail now project minimized immutable call,
proposal and reviewed-seal context under `decision_message`. The source follows
the original decision revision, verifies tenant/case/proposal/definition binding,
and uses the activated definition's immutable name, proposal creation time and
seal number/time. Technical references remain progressively visible. Arbitrary
answers, profiles, other recipients and staff review are not label sources.
Acknowledgement-only reads neither join nor disclose this context. Missing or
incoherent context does not fabricate current information or erase the message.

Detail alone may link to the current proposal after independent self-proposal
admission with the destination's complete relationship-specific field ceiling.
The navigation check does not load a broad catalog or answer/profile projection.
Before/after-render source admission can remove the optional link; the rerender
retains the message and finally rechecks its own authority. A former contributor
keeps their original addressed context without regaining proposal membership.
Existing receipt command/version/retry semantics and historical lifecycle
exceptions remain unchanged. No migration, new writer, current profile, production
route, permission catalog or CI policy changes.

Requirements PRG-004/006/009, AUD-001, QRY-005 through QRY-008 and UX-005 through
UX-008/029; ADR 0085 is refined without reversal. ADR 0100 still defers native
execution. Requirements, page/module/recovery contracts and CHANGELOG are updated.

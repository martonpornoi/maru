## Outcome and boundaries

The ordinary local launcher now opens a short trial with one ongoing and one
future fictional MaruCon. Each has three Announcements and three Guides: draft,
waiting review and approved. Separate Author, Reviewer and Reader accounts make
the relevant boundaries visible. Readers receive approved Guides only; the
existing Announcements staff permission also exposes drafts/history and is not
granted to them. Setup and platform accounts are kept off the practice page.

One captured Europe/Budapest date anchors both timelines. Reloading preserves
edits, dates and history. New invocations allocate a fresh disposable database;
reseed refuses existing data. Real foundation, acceptance, access and content
commands preserve exact authority provenance. The genuine restricted runtime
login prepares content after stopped maintenance activation. No production
schema, adoption profile, guard, permission boundary or acceptance gate changed.
The older v6 dataset remains available explicitly as the legacy scenario.

Public disposable practice credentials are generated in memory and shown only
by the explicitly enabled local page. Copy works while masked, normal sign-in
and CSRF remain required, and an explicit clear button replaces the clipboard.
Operating-system clipboard history/sync cannot be excluded by the browser and
is disclosed. Private acceptance fixtures and their stronger rules are unchanged.

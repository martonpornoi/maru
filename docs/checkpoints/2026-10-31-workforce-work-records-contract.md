## Current evidence and decision

The existing completion command sets the demand and confirmed commitments to
Completed after locked work ends, recording completion actor/time/reason. It does
not record arrival, departure, breaks or another person's handover acceptance.
ADR 0078 explicitly deferred those larger contracts. ADR 0130 extends that future
boundary without changing its accepted planning lifecycle or historical evidence.

New work observations distinguish occurrence time from recording time and their
authenticated source. Unknown remains unknown; no timer manufactures absence,
lateness or a performance conclusion. Actual breaks are explicit. Independent
review, a subject's acknowledgement, agreement and a dispute remain separate.
Handover belongs to the named recipient's exact-version action. Scheduling keeps
personal publication. Minimized manual notes confer no offline authority.

The contract requires actual organization-owned purpose, recipients, reviewed
policy/horizon, retention, holds, subject rights and disposal rules before any
collection. It selects no legal basis or retention duration. Future dedicated
purposes and reviewed profile expansion are required; current permissions gain
no implicit authority. Future writes need closed inputs, optimistic idempotency,
native invariants, atomic evidence and complete-graph recovery.

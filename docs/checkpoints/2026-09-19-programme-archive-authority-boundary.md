## Contract and implementation

0106 immutable two-person operational approval. Requirements EVT-007, INT-007,

than broadening ordinary history fields or existing immutable roles.

`archive_requests` and `source_lineage`. The new `exit-archive@1` recipe supplies
only that permission at Edition scope. All 28 existing definitions/digests and
both roots remain unchanged. The adapter, capability and recipe remain absent
from every current profile and the isolated full-journey manifest. Nothing is
granted or activated by catalog registration.

The public Programme admission helper uses current owner actor/edition references
and real exact-edition policy, closed nonempty field sets and exact profile-adapter
admission. Test-policy replacement retains its existing sealed isolation guard.
It returns only additional-purpose admission: no source read, task, artifact,
grant, audit or current download right. Actual owner collectors and retrieval
must independently authorize contents, current requester, retention, consistency
and audit. Whole-edition composition must establish complete person lock order.

Authorization migrations 0037/0038 extend the exact native minimum-scope function
and literal immutable recipe. Relation schemas and runtime ACLs are unchanged.
Reverse uses exclusive locks and refuses used grant/bundle or recipe request/bundle
evidence before vocabulary contraction. Retain used evidence and fix forward.
Source-derived expected fingerprints and the existing fixture's recipe-helper
source expectation advance together; no new helper privilege or readiness bypass.

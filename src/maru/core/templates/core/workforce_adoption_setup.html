Deliberate boundary

Only Workforce is adopted

This creates the minimum Organization → Convention series → Event edition foundation, then enables Departments, Positions, assignments, Availability, and Shifts. It does not create Registration, tickets, payments, attendance, shops, programme applications, venues, charity, or Logistics records.

If a new organization needs accountable access, Maru creates a Maru operators representation. Those people are responsible for operating this software; the label does not claim that they belong to the organization's legal Executive Board.

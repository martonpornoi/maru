What stopping means

This archives this Programme adoption. It does not cancel the convention or shut down Maru. An active timetable must first be withdrawn through your independent Scheduling authority.

Source records, approval evidence and accountable history remain. Shifts and physical commitments are not completed, cancelled or erased. Shared roles, other editions and platform accounts remain available for their independently authorized purposes. No external notice is sent.

Retained edition rights do not reopen operational writes. Already downloaded files cannot be recalled; disconnected copies are historical and retain their original expiry. Online known-stop verification prevents operational use.

Stop is terminal in ordinary operation. Recovery requires governed fix-forward or a consistent supported restore, not a profile toggle. Archive retrieval remains independently authorized and source-checked.

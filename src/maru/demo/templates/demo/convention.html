A short trial

  1. Author: edit “Preparing the welcome desk,” save it and ask for review.
  2. Reviewer: open the draft and approve it or ask for a change. An author cannot approve their own work.
  3. Reader: find the approved guide and download its text. Drafts and private notes stay hidden.

Authors and Reviewers can also write and approve Announcements. Approved text is ready to copy; approval does not publish it anywhere.

Readers can open approved Guides only. Announcements currently needs staff access, which includes drafts and history, so the Reader has no access to that workspace. Each event has its own accounts. They cannot open the other event's content. The prepared approvals are synthetic practice, not independent human testing.

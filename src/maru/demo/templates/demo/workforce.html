A short trial

  1. Future Reviewer: review the proposed Welcome helper Assignment. Its Manager made the proposal and cannot approve it themselves. Review the requirements and decide using the existing page.
  2. Future Volunteer: open My Availability, review the private draft and choose whether to share it. Organizers cannot see the draft's periods. Sharing an empty plan means deliberately unavailable.
  3. Future Manager: open Shifts, review Future welcome desk and open it for claims. The Volunteer can then claim suitable work in My Shifts; the Reviewer makes the independent confirmation.
  4. Ongoing Manager or Reviewer: open Home and choose this event if needed. Follow the primary suggestion and Other team work. A prepared claim is waiting for review. The short locked practice Shift ends five actual minutes after it was created; refresh Home after that time to see its completion review.
  5. Ongoing Manager: review the ended locked Shift and choose Complete when appropriate. This records planned-work closeout, without claiming attendance or a human handover.

Each account belongs to one event. The Reviewer can review Assignments and Shifts but cannot edit team structure. The Volunteer has self-service access and the minimal role from an approved Assignment. Platform/setup accounts are not displayed. Prepared decisions are synthetic setup, not independent human acceptance.

If a decision asks you to confirm it is you, use the current practice role's password again. After confirming, review the record and submit your decision.

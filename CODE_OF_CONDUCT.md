# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes;
- focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki
edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of
Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maru currently has one maintainer and does not provide a private
project-specific conduct-reporting channel or an independent internal reviewer.
Do not put harassment reports, identities, or other sensitive personal
information in a public issue or Discussion. Do not use the private
vulnerability-reporting form for ordinary conduct reports.

For abusive content or behavior hosted on GitHub that may violate GitHub's
policies, use
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports sent to GitHub Support through that process are received and decided by
GitHub, not Maru. That route does not cover every Maru-specific rule or off-
platform interaction.

The maintainer may moderate conduct visible in Maru's public project spaces
under this Code, but Maru cannot accept confidential conduct reports, provide
independent review of a concern involving the maintainer, or promise an
acknowledgment, investigation, or resolution time. This limitation remains
public until the project establishes an operational private channel and
independent review capacity.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines:

1. **Correction** — A written warning through an appropriate available channel
   for inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or
   unwelcome. Maru does not solicit private contact details solely to deliver a
   warning; when no appropriate channel exists, the maintainer may remove the
   content or use GitHub's moderation controls.
2. **Warning** — A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including
   no interaction with involved people for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — A temporary ban from interaction or public communication
   with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — Permanent removal for a pattern of violations, harassment,
   aggression, or disparagement of protected classes.
